Our story

Built to move Edmonton, one delivery at a time.

Central Courier Services was established in 2005 with a vision to transform the logistics and dispatch sector. We aim to deliver seamless, efficient, and economical logistics solutions — using technology and innovation to make every delivery more reliable and more transparent than the last.

Headquartered in Edmonton, Alberta, we provide worldwide logistics support throughout Edmonton and the surrounding regions — with a dispatch team that treats every parcel like it's the only one on the route.
